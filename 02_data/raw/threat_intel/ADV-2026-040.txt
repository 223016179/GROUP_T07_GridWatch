Advisory ADV-2026-040
Threat actor / activity cluster: Unattributed activity cluster (tracked internally as CLUSTER-COBALT)

Summary:
A cluster of intrusions against distribution-utility engineering networks has been observed beginning with phishing emails delivering a macro-enabled document, followed by execution of a living-off-the-land binary to establish persistence via a scheduled task on the initial foothold host before any attempt to move toward OT segments.

Associated ATT&CK for ICS tactics: Initial Access, Execution, Persistence

Indicators observed:
- macro-enabled document delivered via email
- scheduled task created outside change window
- living-off-the-land binary invoked with encoded arguments

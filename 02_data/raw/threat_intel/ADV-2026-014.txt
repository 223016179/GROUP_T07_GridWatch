Advisory ADV-2026-014
Threat actor / activity cluster: Unattributed activity cluster (tracked internally as CLUSTER-BRONZE)

Summary:
Analysts observed an activity cluster abusing legitimate third-party remote-maintenance accounts to reach engineering workstations at energy-sector operators. Initial access relied on valid vendor credentials rather than exploitation, followed by credential-access tooling and staged collection of engineering-network files prior to attempts to reach historian and control-adjacent hosts.

Associated ATT&CK for ICS tactics: Initial Access, Credential Access, Collection, Lateral Movement

Indicators observed:
- off-hours vendor logons
- atypical source IP for known vendor account
- credential-dumping utility execution
- archive-staging utility on engineering host

Advisory ADV-2026-081
Threat actor / activity cluster: Unattributed activity cluster (tracked internally as CLUSTER-COBALT)

Summary:
CLUSTER-COBALT activity culminated, in one documented case, in an attempted impact against process availability through repeated unauthorized controller mode changes, though the attempt was interrupted before any sustained disruption occurred.

Associated ATT&CK for ICS tactics: Impair Process Control, Impact

Indicators observed:
- unauthorized controller mode change
- repeated mode-change attempts within short interval

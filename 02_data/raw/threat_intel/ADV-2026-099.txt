Advisory ADV-2026-099
Threat actor / activity cluster: Unattributed

Summary:
Operators are advised that persistence in several reported intrusions was achieved through creation of a new local account on the engineering workstation used as the initial vendor access point, rather than through malware requiring ongoing execution.

Associated ATT&CK for ICS tactics: Persistence

Indicators observed:
- new local account created on engineering workstation
- account creation outside documented change request

Advisory ADV-2026-069
Threat actor / activity cluster: Unattributed

Summary:
Analysts documented an intrusion in which the actor disabled automated alerting on a security information and event management console prior to escalating access, an inhibit-response-function technique intended to delay defender detection during later stages.

Associated ATT&CK for ICS tactics: Inhibit Response Function, Defense Evasion

Indicators observed:
- SIEM alert rule disabled by non-administrative account
- gap in alert volume preceding confirmed compromise window
